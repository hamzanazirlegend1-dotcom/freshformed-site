Questions

Where does the data come from?
The official Companies House public register, through their API. Every row links to the register entry so you can check it.
Is this personal data?
No. We send company details only: name, number, type, SIC codes, registered office. We do not include directors, shareholders or anyone's home address. How you contact a company is your responsibility under PECR and GDPR; a letter to the registered office is the usual approach.
How fresh is it?
Each morning's email covers companies incorporated the previous working day. Monday's email covers Friday (and any weekend registrations).
Can I change my filter?
Yes, as often as you like. Reply to any alert email and it is changed the same day.
What if a day has no matches?
You still get a short email so you know the service ran, unless you ask us to skip empty days.
How do I cancel?
From your Payhip account page, or reply to any email. No notice period.
